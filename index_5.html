⚙️ Pool Configuration

Configure your Gist URL and participant assignments. All visitors share the same data via your GitHub Gist.

Step 1: Create a GitHub Gist

Create a public Gist at gist.github.com with filename pool2026.json and the content {}. Then paste your Gist's raw URL below.

Raw URL format:

https://gist.githubusercontent.com/YOUR_USERNAME/GIST_ID/raw/pool2026.json

Step 2: Enter Participant Assignments

For each participant, enter their name and assign one team per seed tier. Teams are auto-populated from the 2026 bracket.
